Section 01

Information We Collect

To book, service and warranty-administer your motorcycle we need a surprisingly small file on you. We collect:

  • Contact identity: name, phone number, email address provided at booking or enquiry.
  • Machine data: make, model, year, VIN, mileage history and parts fitted to your specific bike.
  • Payment confirmation: card brand and last four digits only. Full numbers are processed by our PCI-DSS compliant processor and never touch our systems.
  • Workshop imagery: photos of components taken during your job, used for estimates, claims and your report folder.

We do not collect special-category data, we do not run advertising trackers, and we do not buy contact lists from anyone.

Section 02

How We Use It & Cookies

Your file exists to serve your bike. Concretely: confirming appointments, producing written estimates, ordering correct parts, administering manufacturer warranties, sending service reminders tied to your actual mileage, and — if you opt in — one monthly newsletter.

This site stores two preferences locally in your browser (light/dark theme and text direction) using localStorage. These never leave your device. We also load privacy-respecting, cookie-less analytics that show us page popularity without identifying visitors. No cross-site tracking, no ad pixels, no fingerprinting.

Newsletter consent is granular and revocable from any email footer; booking-related communications are considered part of the service you requested and can't be opted out of while a job is active.

Section 03

Sharing & Security

Your data is shared only where the job physically requires it: parts suppliers receive your bike's details (never your payment info), manufacturers receive warranty documentation you've asked us to file, and our insurance partner receives incident reports when applicable. We have never sold data, and contracts with every vendor prohibit it too.

On security: records live in an encrypted cloud workspace with hardware-key multi-factor authentication; the workshop network is segmented from guest Wi-Fi; paper files sit in a locked cabinet behind Bay 03's office door; access is role-limited to staff who need it. Breaches, should one ever occur, will be disclosed within 72 hours.

Section 04

Your Rights

You hold the wrench on your own data. At any time you may request a copy of everything we hold (emailed within five working days), correction of anything wrong, deletion of anything we're not legally obliged to keep, or export in machine-readable format. You may also object to marketing outright and restrict processing during a dispute.

We don't use your data for automated decisions that affect you — every quote, approval and warranty outcome involves an actual person who can explain the reasoning over coffee.

Section 05

Retention & Changes

Service records are kept for seven years after your last visit — they materially protect both of us in warranty and resale contexts. Enquiries that never become bookings are deleted after twelve months. CCTV at the shop entrance rolls over after thirty days.

When this policy changes materially, we email registered customers at least fourteen days before the new version takes effect, and previous versions remain available on request.

Section 06

Contact Our Privacy Lead

Data questions go straight to Maya Torres, our designated privacy lead: privacy@apexmotoworks.com, or by post at 217 Torque Avenue, Unit 9, Chicago, IL 60616. Every request gets a human answer within five working days — usually faster than the coffee machine refills.
