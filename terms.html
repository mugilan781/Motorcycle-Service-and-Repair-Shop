Section 01

Service Scope & Estimates

All work is performed against a written estimate that itemises labour at $110/hour plus parts at invoice cost. Estimates are valid for fourteen days and assume the motorcycle matches the description provided at booking. Teardown work (engine internals, suspension internals, electrical fault-tracing) is quoted as a diagnostic stage; findings from teardown may change the final figure, in which case work pauses until you approve a revised estimate.

Published package prices cover the listed scope only. Consumables — oil volumes beyond four litres, tyres, brake pads, chains, batteries — are quoted separately at cost because they vary by machine. We never substitute brands without written approval, and any part you supply carries no workmanship warranty from us.

Section 02

Bookings, Cancellation & No-Shows

Appointments may be rescheduled or cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before the slot. Jobs estimated above $800 require a 30% deposit to reserve parts and bay time; deposits are refundable on the same 24-hour basis or convertible to store credit indefinitely. Repeated no-shows (three within twelve months) move future bookings to a prepaid basis — we'll always tell you before applying this.

If we miss a promised handback time for reasons within our control, labour on the affected job is reduced by 10%. If your bike is kept longer by your own delay in approving additional work, storage terms under §04 begin after seven days.

Section 03

Warranty & Liability

Workmanship carries a 24-month / 24,000 km warranty: if our fitting, adjustment or diagnosis fails, we make it right at no labour charge, at your convenience. Genuine parts retain their full manufacturer warranty, which we administer for you. Customer-supplied parts, race-only modifications, machines used in competitive events after a street-spec job, and normal wear items are excluded.

Our liability for any claim connected to services rendered is limited to the amount you paid for the affected work. We are not liable for consequential losses (track fees, missed tours, rental bikes). Nothing in these terms limits liability where doing so would be unlawful, and your statutory consumer rights remain fully intact.

Section 04

Your Machine & Stored Vehicles

You confirm the motorcycle is yours or that you act with the owner's authority; we may request photo ID matching registration for high-value jobs. Please remove personal items — helmets, documents, toll transponders — before drop-off; we photograph valuables left aboard but can't accept responsibility for them.

Completed bikes wait under covered storage free for seven days, then accrue $8/day (first thirty days) while remaining fully insured on our floor. After sixty days of no contact, Illinois abandoned-vehicle procedures apply, and we'd genuinely rather never meet them — reply to any reminder email and we'll sort it out like humans.

Section 06

Governing Law, Disputes & Changes

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Illinois. If something goes wrong, talk to us first — every dispute in eighteen years has ended with coffee and a resolution, not lawyers. Unresolved matters fall to small-claims court or arbitration in Cook County, whichever you prefer.

We may update these terms as services evolve; material changes are emailed to customers fourteen days ahead, and the version above is always the one currently in force.
