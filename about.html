Our Story

One Dyno, One Lift,
An Unreasonable Standard

Apex began as a two-bay garage where founder Marco Bellini refused to return a single bike he wouldn't ride himself. Eighteen years later the floor is bigger — the refusal isn't.

Today we run six bays, an in-house dyno cell and a diagnostics lab, yet every machine still leaves with a signed inspection sheet and a phone call Marco would be proud of.

Marco Bellini

Founder & Master Technician

Milestones

Eighteen Years,
Measured in Torque

From one lift to a full performance centre — the road so far.

  1. 2008

    Two Bays, One Promise

    Marco opens Apex with a single lift and a hand-painted sign: "Fixed right, or it's free."

  2. 2012

    The Dyno Cell Arrives

    A 320hp inertia dyno turns opinions into data sheets. Tune work triples overnight.

  3. 2016

    Factory Certifications

    Ducati Service Point and BMW Motorrad certification — dealer capability, independent soul.

  4. 2020

    Diagnostics Lab 2.0

    Oscilloscopes, ECU benches and live-sensor tracing replace guesswork forever.

  5. 2024

    Service No. 24,500

    A 1998 Fireblade rolls out fully rebuilt — our twenty-four thousandth signature on an inspection sheet.
